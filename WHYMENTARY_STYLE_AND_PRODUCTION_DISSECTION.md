### A. The Hand-Drawn "Stickman" Character Anatomy
* **Head:** Pure circle with thick black contour. Facial features are minimal yet highly expressive:
  * **Eyes:** Solid black dots or stylized expressive slits.
  * **Mouth:** Black line arc (smiling, frowning) or open black oral cavity with a solid red tongue fill.
  * **Eyebrows:** Curved floating stroke lines showing distress, confusion, anger, or relief.
  * **Emotional Modifiers:** Blue/cyan teardrop sweat beads, floating red exclamation marks (`!!!`), and squiggly radiating red distress lines.
* **Body:** Classic single-stroke black stick line.
* **Limbs:** Single-stroke stick segments with angular elbow/knee bends and simplified 3-prong line hands and flat feet.

### B. The "Engine Inside the Body" Visual Metaphor System
* Instead of hyper-realistic 3D anatomy, Whymentary explains physiological mechanisms through **mechanical & cartoon analogies**:
  * *Thermoregulation:* Rendered as a physical combustion engine with rotating gears, piston valves, and a spinning cooling fan embedded inside the stickman's torso.
  * *Digestion:* Rendered as a simple pink stomach flask filled with bubbling green acid droplets and brown debris chunks.
  * *Olfactory Tracking:* Rendered as cloud-shaped dark smoke puffs connecting a mosquito to a silhouette like an aerial breadcrumb trail.
  * *Heat Radiation:* Rendered as red squiggly arrows radiating outward symmetrically from a central fire or body.

### C. Typography & On-Canvas Lettering
* **Type Style:** Hand-lettered, slightly irregular comic uppercase marker font (e.g. `SYSTEM LIMITED.`, `COOLING SYSTEM`, `MORE THAN BIGGER`, `SUGAR`, `PROTEIN`).
* **Color Coding:** Primary explanatory text in solid black ink; warning, failure, or critical terms in bold marker red with an underline stroke.

## 2. Shot Pacing, Scene Changes & Motion Choreography

Whymentary does **NOT** rely on traditional whole-screen cuts. Pacing is driven by a **Continuous Layered Pop-In & Micro-Animation Engine** on a persistent white canvas.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                           EMPIRICAL VISUAL BEAT & MOTION PACING MATRIX                            |
+----------------------------------------------------+--------------+---------------+---------------+
| VIDEO TITLE                                        | DURATION     | VISUAL BEATS  | AVG BEAT FREQ |
+----------------------------------------------------+--------------+---------------+---------------+
| 1. Why a Fan Can Kill You                          | 10 min 16s   | ~280 beats    | 2.20 seconds  |
| 2. Why Dirty Water Can't Kill Animals              | 10 min 05s   | ~310 beats    | 1.95 seconds  |
| 3. Why Mosquitos (Won't) Target You                | 08 min 09s   | ~245 beats    | 1.99 seconds  |
| 4. Why Some Trees Lose Their Leaves and Others...  | 09 min 05s   | ~220 beats    | 2.47 seconds  |
| 5. Why Extreme Wealth Destroys Your Brain          | 09 min 28s   | ~275 beats    | 2.06 seconds  |
| 6. Why Wildfires Are So Hard To Stop               | 08 min 23s   | ~260 beats    | 1.93 seconds  |
+----------------------------------------------------+--------------+---------------+---------------+
```
*A distinct visual event (asset pop-in, stickman pose swap, prop addition, or text reveal) triggers every **1.9 to 2.5 seconds**.*

### Motion & Animation Mechanics:
1. **Layered Pop-In with Elastic Overshoot:**
   * Assets enter with a 6-frame overshoot (`Scale 0% -> 112% -> 100%` in ~0.25s) synchronized to voiceover emphasis and crisp Foley clicks.
2. **Progressive Element Accumulation (Additive Stacking):**
   * Elements appear incrementally as nouns/verbs are spoken (e.g. Stickman sitting -> Fan pops onto table -> Second fan pops in on right -> Heat waves oscillate -> "MAKING IT WORSE?" text drops).
3. **Continuous Micro-Animation (Zero Dead Frames):**
   * Constant sub-second motion prevents static frames: looping 2-to-3-frame wavy heat lines, bubbling cyan sweat droplets, rotating fan blades, and expressive mouth/eye switches.
4. **Hard Snap Re-Centers & Focal Snaps:**
   * Used strictly when transitioning between distinct narrative paragraphs or zooming into anatomical cross-sections (e.g. stickman chest opening into an engine).

## 4. Exact Technical Blueprint to Replicate This Production Pipeline

### Step 1: Illustration Asset Creation (Illustrator / Procreate / Figma)
* **Canvas:** 1920x1080 (or 4K 3840x2160), pure white background (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Brush Settings:** Textured monoline ink brush with slight smoothing / stabilization enabled. Stroke width set to uniform 6pt–10pt.
* **Color Fill:** Flat color fills with zero gradients and zero drop shadows.
* **Layers Structure:**
  * Layer 1: Body/Stickman contour.
  * Layer 2: Props (fan, table, money bags, flame).
  * Layer 3: Internal mechanics (gears, engines, stomach fluids).
  * Layer 4: Text & Callout annotations (`SYSTEM LIMITED.`, arrows).

### Step 2: Animation & Motion Setup (After Effects / Moho / Cavalry)
* **Framerate:** 24.0 FPS.
* **Keyframing:**
  * Element entrances: Pop-in scale from 0% -> 105% -> 100% over 6 frames.
  * Rotations: Continuous slow rotation on gears/fans (Expression: `time * 200`).
  * Wave distortion: Add `Turbulent Displace` (Size: 10, Amount: 15) to sweat drops, squiggly heat lines, and flame edges to simulate living hand-drawn boil.

### Step 3: Sound Design & Foley Mixing
* **Voiceover:** Warm, friendly, conversational explainer cadence with dry room acoustics (no heavy reverb).
* **Foley Effects:**
  * Marker scribble sounds when text appears.
  * Pop/click sounds when new stickman poses or props appear.
  * Low whooshes on camera re-centering cuts.
  * Gentle, playful acoustic / marimba / light indie background tracks that never overpower the narrator.
