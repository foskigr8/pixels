# style.md — the look

This file is quoted directly into critique criteria, so write it as things that
can be *checked in a single frame*, not as vibes. "Cold blue shadows with warm
practical lights" is checkable. "Cinematic and epic" is not.

Replace everything below with your actual direction.

---

## One-line summary

> A moody, rain-slicked near-future city at night, shot like a 90s thriller.

## Palette

- Base: desaturated blue-greys, near-black shadows
- Accent: sodium-orange practical lights, neon magenta reflections
- Never: bright saturated greens, pastel anything

## Light

- Single dominant key from a practical source in-frame, hard-edged
- Deep falloff — the background should go to near-black, not grey
- Wet surfaces everywhere; reflections carry most of the colour

## Camera

- 35mm equivalent, eye level or slightly low
- Shallow depth of field, background clearly separated
- Slight anamorphic-style horizontal flare on strong lights

## Texture and rendering

- Photographic, fine film grain
- No illustration, no 3D-render look, no plastic skin
- Slight halation around highlights

## Composition rules

- Subject off-centre, roughly on a third
- Strong foreground element for depth in at least half the shots
- Horizon level unless the shot notes say otherwise

## Hard no-list

These are the things to fail a frame for:

- Text or signage that reads as garbled letterforms
- More than five fingers, broken limbs, melted faces
- Flat, evenly-lit scenes with no visible key
- Modern car logos or recognisable real-world branding
- Anything that looks like stock photography
